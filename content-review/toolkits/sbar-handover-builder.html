SBAR Handover Builder

Structure a safe verbal handover to your registrar.

Educational aid only. This is a personal reference and survival resource. It does not replace senior clinical advice, local trust policies, professional judgment, or emergency escalation procedures.
Situation — who you are, patient age/sex, location, and the one-line problem.
Background — relevant past medical history, anticoagulation, allergies, current treatment.
Assessment — observations / NEWS2, airway status, key examination findings, imaging, bloods, working diagnosis.
Recommendation — what you think and what you want: 'I think…' / 'I'd like…'.

Escalation

Be ready to answer: What worries you most? What do you want me to do?
