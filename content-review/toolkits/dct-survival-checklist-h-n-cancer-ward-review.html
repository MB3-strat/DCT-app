DCT Survival Checklist — H&N Cancer Ward Review

Twenty checks before you leave the bedside or hand over.

Educational aid only. This is a personal reference and survival resource. It does not replace senior clinical advice, local trust policies, professional judgment, or emergency escalation procedures.
Do I know what operation the patient had?
Do I know whether they have a tracheostomy or laryngectomy?
Is the airway currently safe?
Is the flap viable?
Has the flap changed from its previous assessment?
Is there any neck swelling?
Are the drains functioning?
What is the drain output?
Could this be a chyle leak?
Could this be a salivary/pharyngeal leak?
Is the wound healthy?
Is the patient adequately hydrated?
Are electrolytes satisfactory?
Is nutrition appropriate?
Is the patient receiving appropriate VTE prophylaxis?
Is respiratory function satisfactory?
Is pain controlled?
Is the patient mobilising?
Are there any new concerns?
Does the registrar/consultant need to know?
