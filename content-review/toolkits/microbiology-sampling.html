Microbiology Sampling

Take the right sample, before antibiotics.

Educational aid only. This is a personal reference and survival resource. It does not replace senior clinical advice, local trust policies, professional judgment, or emergency escalation procedures.
Blood cultures
Pus swab
Deep tissue specimen
Bone biopsy
Aspirate
Saliva
Cultures taken before antibiotics where possible
Sample labelled correctly
Clinical details included on the request
Antibiotic timing documented

Escalation

Discuss with senior / microbiology if deep infection, sepsis, immunosuppression, unusual organism risk or failure to improve.
