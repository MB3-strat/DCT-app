🚑 ALGORITHM Emergency T19

ABCDE + FLAP Rapid Review

What has changed from this patient's previous baseline?

Educational aid only. This is a personal reference and survival resource. It does not replace senior clinical advice, local trust policies, professional judgment, or emergency escalation procedures.
1

A — Airway: Is it patent? Is the patient talking? Is there stridor or airway obstruction? Is the tracheostomy patent, or has the tube been displaced? Is there blood or secretions obstructing the tube? Is this a laryngectomy patient?

2

B — Breathing: Is oxygenation/ventilation adequate? Check respiratory rate, oxygen saturations, work of breathing, chest movement, breath sounds and oxygen requirement.

3

C — Circulation: Is the patient perfused and not bleeding? Check heart rate, blood pressure, capillary refill, peripheral perfusion, bleeding, drain output, neck swelling and urine output.

4

D — Disability: Is neurological status unchanged? Check conscious level, confusion, pain and blood glucose if appropriate.

5

E — Exposure: What is happening to the wound, neck and donor site? Examine the neck, flap, wound, drains, donor site, tracheostomy, chest, abdomen and legs for DVT signs.

6

+ FLAP: Is the reconstruction viable?

7

F — Feeding: What is the nutritional plan?

8

L — Losses: What are the drain / fluid losses?

9

A — Antibiotics / Analgesia / Anticoagulation: Are the medications appropriate?

10

P — Plan: What happens next?
