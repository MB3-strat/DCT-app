Consent Checklist

Montgomery-compliant consent for OMFS procedures.

Educational aid only. This is a personal reference and survival resource. It does not replace senior clinical advice, local trust policies, professional judgment, or emergency escalation procedures.
Diagnosis explained in patient's language
Procedure explained step-by-step
Material risks discussed (individualised)
Common risks: pain, bleeding, swelling, infection
Specific risks: nerve injury (IAN, lingual, mental)
Alternatives including no treatment discussed
Recovery expectations set
Questions invited and answered
Written consent signed and copy given

Escalation

If capacity is uncertain or the procedure is complex, discuss with your senior before proceeding.
