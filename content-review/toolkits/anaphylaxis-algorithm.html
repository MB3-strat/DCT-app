💉 ALGORITHM Emergency T04

Anaphylaxis Algorithm

Resuscitation Council UK — act immediately.

Educational aid only. This is a personal reference and survival resource. It does not replace senior clinical advice, local trust policies, professional judgment, or emergency escalation procedures.
1

Call for help. Remove trigger. Lie flat, elevate legs.

2

IM Adrenaline 500 micrograms (0.5 mL 1:1000) — anterolateral mid-thigh.

3

High-flow oxygen 15L. IV fluid bolus 500–1000 mL crystalloid.

4

Repeat IM adrenaline every 5 min if no improvement.

5

Chlorphenamine 10 mg IV, Hydrocortisone 200 mg IV (second-line).

6

Serial obs. Refractory → adrenaline infusion, ICU.

Escalation

Refractory anaphylaxis — call anaesthetics / critical care for adrenaline infusion and ICU.
