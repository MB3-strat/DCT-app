🚑 ALGORITHM Emergency T02

ABCDE Assessment

Life-support primary survey. Do not skip a step.

Educational aid only. This is a personal reference and survival resource. It does not replace senior clinical advice, local trust policies, professional judgment, or emergency escalation procedures.
1

A — Airway: patent? talking? stridor? soft-tissue swelling? Head-tilt / jaw-thrust.

2

B — Breathing: RR, SpO2, chest expansion, auscultate. High-flow O2 15L via non-rebreather.

3

C — Circulation: HR, BP, cap refill, IV access ×2 large-bore, bloods + gas.

4

D — Disability: AVPU / GCS, pupils, glucose (Don't Ever Forget Glucose).

5

E — Exposure: full survey, temperature, control haemorrhage. Reassess A→B→C.

Escalation

Any compromise at any step — call for senior help and reassess from A.
