🩸 CHECKLIST Emergency T03

Sepsis 6

Complete within 1 hour of recognising sepsis.

Educational aid only. This is a personal reference and survival resource. It does not replace senior clinical advice, local trust policies, professional judgment, or emergency escalation procedures.
Give high-flow oxygen
Take blood cultures (before antibiotics if possible)
Give IV broad-spectrum antibiotics
Give IV fluid resuscitation
Measure serum lactate
Measure accurate hourly urine output

Escalation

Escalate to registrar and critical care early if lactate raised or no response to fluids.
