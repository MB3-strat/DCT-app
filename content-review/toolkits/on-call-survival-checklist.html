On-Call Survival Checklist

Set yourself up for a safe shift.

Educational aid only. This is a personal reference and survival resource. It does not replace senior clinical advice, local trust policies, professional judgment, or emergency escalation procedures.
Referral received and documented
ABCDE assessment completed
History taken
Examination completed
Bloods reviewed / requested
Imaging reviewed / requested
Senior informed where indicated
Plan documented
Analgesia prescribed / reviewed
Antibiotics prescribed / reviewed where indicated
Follow-up arranged
Safety netting documented

Escalation

If airway, haemorrhage, sepsis, visual symptoms, deterioration or uncertainty are present, call senior help early.
