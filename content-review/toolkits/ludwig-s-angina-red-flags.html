Ludwig's Angina Red Flags

Any tick = call anaesthetics + ENT NOW.

Educational aid only. This is a personal reference and survival resource. It does not replace senior clinical advice, local trust policies, professional judgment, or emergency escalation procedures.
Bilateral submandibular swelling
Elevated floor of mouth / tongue displacement
Drooling or inability to swallow saliva
Voice change / muffled 'hot potato' voice
Stridor or noisy breathing
Tripod posture / can't lie flat
SpO2 falling on room air
Trismus reducing airway access

Escalation

Any positive finding — call anaesthetics and ENT immediately; this is an airway emergency.
