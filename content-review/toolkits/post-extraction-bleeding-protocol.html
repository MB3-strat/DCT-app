Post-Extraction Bleeding Protocol

Stepwise haemostasis for the bleeding socket.

Educational aid only. This is a personal reference and survival resource. It does not replace senior clinical advice, local trust policies, professional judgment, or emergency escalation procedures.
1

ABCDE. Sit patient up. Suction and inspect the socket.

2

Firm gauze pressure over socket for 20 minutes.

3

LA with adrenaline infiltrated into socket margins.

4

Debride granulation tissue. Consider haemostatic agent (Surgicel).

5

Suture socket with resorbable (3/0 or 4/0 Vicryl Rapide).

6

Tranexamic acid mouthwash 5% 10 mL QDS × 5 days.

7

Check FBC, INR/APTT, check meds (DOACs, warfarin, antiplatelets). Escalate.

Escalation

Escalate to registrar if bleeding is uncontrolled, patient is anticoagulated, or haemodynamically unstable.
