Major Trauma Checklist

OMFS role in a polytrauma call.

Educational aid only. This is a personal reference and survival resource. It does not replace senior clinical advice, local trust policies, professional judgment, or emergency escalation procedures.
ATLS principles followed
Airway assessed
C-spine considered / protected
Breathing assessed
Circulation assessed
Disability assessed
Exposure completed
Facial examination completed
Occlusion checked
Eye examination completed
CT requested / reviewed
Other injuries considered
Specialty referrals made
Documentation completed

Escalation

Escalate immediately to trauma team / OMFS registrar for airway concern, bleeding, visual signs, GCS drop, polytrauma or unstable observations.
