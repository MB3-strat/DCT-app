Common Blood Tests

What to interpret at 3 AM.

Educational aid only. This is a personal reference and survival resource. It does not replace senior clinical advice, local trust policies, professional judgment, or emergency escalation procedures.
FBC reviewed
U&E reviewed
CRP reviewed
LFT reviewed
Coagulation reviewed
Glucose reviewed
Bone profile reviewed where relevant
Group and Save sent where bleeding / theatre risk
Crossmatch requested where significant bleeding risk
Blood cultures sent where infection / sepsis suspected
Raised WCC / neutrophilia interpreted
Raised CRP interpreted
Hyponatraemia considered
Acute kidney injury considered

Escalation

Escalate abnormal bloods that change urgency: sepsis markers, AKI, coagulopathy, severe anaemia, electrolyte disturbance or deteriorating trend.
